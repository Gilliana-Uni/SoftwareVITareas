Reclaiming Your Time & Focus

1. Digital Minimalism

Intentionally reduce screen time and remove addictive apps. Replace endless scrolling with deep work sessions.

2. The "Portfolio First" Rule

Before opening any game or social media, spend at least 90 minutes working on your professional portfolio or skills.

3. Dopamine Detox

Schedule intentional low-stimulation periods. Read books, sketch, code, or go for walks without your phone.
